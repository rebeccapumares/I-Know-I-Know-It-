I know it, I know it…

You can see the word, feel its first letter, and still not say it. This network reads one recall attempt (the language, the word, how long the search took, how sure the person felt, and whether they recognized the word later) and predicts whether that moment was a tip-of-the-tongue state.

Running the trained 22 → 32 → 3 network live in your browser with its exact weights. Trained and tested on a simulated multilingual dataset grounded in published TOT findings.

Built from scratch in NumPy: dense layers, ReLU, dropout, softmax cross-entropy with class weights, Adam, early stopping and gradient checking. The dataset is simulated, with effects taken from TOT research such as more TOTs for rare words and in a second language. Real data in the same format plugs straight in.
